Research Agenda

My research spans agricultural economics, commodity markets, agribusiness, agricultural finance, and industrial organization.

Commodity Markets

Interest rates, information releases, government programs, forecasting, and price dynamics in agricultural commodity markets.

Agribusiness & Industrial Organization

Technology adoption, markups, profitability, consolidation, and market power in agricultural input and food industries.

Agricultural Finance

Credit-market structure, bank competition, relationship lending, and agricultural lending responses to farm-sector conditions.

Policy & Information

How policy announcements and public information affect expectations, market behavior, and agricultural outcomes.
