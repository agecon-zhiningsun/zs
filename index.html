I am an Assistant Professor and E. Koller Chair in Agricultural Information Systems in the Department of Applied Economics at the University of Minnesota. My research interests include agricultural economics, commodity markets, agribusiness, agricultural finance, and industrial organization. I earned my Ph.D. from the Department of Agricultural, Environmental, and Development Economics at The Ohio State University, an M.S. in Environmental Economics and Urban Planning from Tufts University, and dual B.A. degrees in Economics and Financial Management from the University of Minnesota Morris. My email is sunx0636@umn.edu.
